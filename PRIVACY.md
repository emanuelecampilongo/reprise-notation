# Reprise Notation Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Emanuele Campilongo develops Reprise Notation and is responsible for personal information you send directly for support. For privacy questions or requests, email [info@emanuelecampilongo.com](mailto:info@emanuelecampilongo.com).

## Local scores and settings

You can create, edit, play, and save scores locally without a Reprise Notation account. The app stores your preferences, recent file information, session data, backups, and recovery files on your device. Reprise Notation does not send local scores or diagnostics to the developer automatically. The Microsoft Store build does not use developer-operated advertising, usage analytics, or automatic crash reporting. You can manage your local scores, exports, and backups on your device.

## Microsoft Store

The Store edition checks Microsoft Store for app updates at startup. Microsoft processes information needed for installation and update checks, which may include Microsoft account, device, package, and connection information. The app does not send scores as part of an update check. Microsoft handles that processing under the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/privacy/privacystatement).

## Optional third-party services

If you use MuseScore.com from the app, it can receive account and connection information, score and audio files you choose to upload, and related metadata such as titles and sharing settings. Audio.com receives audio and related account or upload information when you choose to share there. The app can store sign-in tokens and cached online scores on your device. You can sign out in the app and manage published content, visibility, and account data with the corresponding service. See the [MuseScore.com Privacy Policy](https://musescore.com/legal/privacy) and the privacy information on [Audio.com](https://audio.com/).

MuseSounds features can contact MuseHub to browse available sounds or load related resources. With a compatible MuseSampler installation, Reprise Notation can contact MuseHub to check the sampler version at startup or when opening a score. Those requests may disclose connection data such as your IP address and operating system. Opening an external help or service link is subject to the site you visit. See the [MuseHub Privacy Notice](https://www.musehub.com/privacy).

## Support correspondence

If you email the developer, your email address, message, and attachments are used to answer your request, diagnose reported issues, and follow up when needed. Sending them is voluntary. The developer relies on a legitimate interest in providing support and maintaining the app. The email provider processes messages to deliver and store them. The developer keeps correspondence for as long as needed to resolve and follow up on the request, or longer if required by law or necessary to handle a dispute. You can review diagnostic files before attaching them.

Where applicable, you may ask to access, correct, or delete information the developer holds about you, restrict its processing, or object to it. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. Use the privacy contact above for requests about support correspondence, and contact the applicable service for data held by Microsoft, MuseScore.com, Audio.com, or MuseHub.
